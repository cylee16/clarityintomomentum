The basics

What health coaching actually is (and isn’t)

Health coaching isn’t someone handing you another plan to follow. It’s a partnership. I don’t stand over you with a clipboard; I sit beside you and help you find the answers that are already in you.

We work on the whole picture — not just food or exercise, but energy, stress, purpose, relationships, and the beliefs quietly running underneath it all. It’s forward-looking (unlike therapy, which often looks back to heal), and it’s built entirely around your real life.

Coaching support, not medical care — I’ll always point you to a doctor or therapist when that’s what’s needed.

Local, but virtual

Why work with a coach who knows the valley?

I live here. I know what winter feels like in the valley, what the pace of life is like, the particular weight of trying to hold it all together in a close-knit community where everyone seems fine.

But here’s the freeing part: because we meet on Zoom, “local” doesn’t mean you have to drive anywhere. You get a coach who understands your world and the convenience of meeting from your own kitchen table — after the kids are down, on your lunch break, wherever works.

I serve women throughout Cache Valley — Logan, North Logan, Smithfield, Providence, Hyrum, Nibley — and across Utah.
